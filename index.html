📖 UK Crypto Tax Rules (2025/26)

CGT rates on crypto (non-property assets): Basic rate taxpayers pay 18% on gains. Higher and additional rate taxpayers pay 24%.

Annual exempt amount: The first £3,000 of gains each tax year is free of CGT (2025/26 and 2024/25).

What counts as a disposal: Selling crypto for cash, swapping one crypto for another, spending crypto on goods/services, and gifting crypto (except to a spouse) are all taxable disposals.

Losses: Capital losses can be offset against gains in the same or future tax years. Report them to HMRC within 4 years.
